Where the medals come from

  • Sport — podium derived from completed matches: gold for the champion, silver for the runner-up, bronze for the third-placed team or participant.
  • Judged — podium taken from the weighted standings of a competition, unless medal awards were entered manually. Socio Cultural events show provisional medals as soon as a round has standings.
  • Award — gold, silver, and bronze awards assigned through the awards page always take precedence.
  • Rows marked PROV come from a competition that is still running; they become final once its status is set to Completed. Draft and cancelled competitions never count.
